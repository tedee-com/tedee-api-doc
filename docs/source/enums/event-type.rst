Event type
---------------------------------------

This enum describes possible events in device activity logs

| Number |            Name            |                                                    Description                                                     |
+========+============================+====================================================================================================================+
| 32     | LockedRemote               | Locked via mobile app                                                                                              |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 33     | UnlockedRemote             | Unlocked via mobile app                                                                                            |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 34     | LockedButton               | Locked by pressing the button on lock device                                                                       |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 35     | UnlockedButton             | Unlocked by pressing the button on lock device                                                                     |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 36     | LockedByAutoLock           | Locked by auto lock feature                                                                                        |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 37     | UnlockedByAutoUnlock       | Unlocked by auto unlock feature (lock was in locked or partially locked position)                                  |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 38     | LockedManual               | Lock was rotated manually into locked position                                                                     |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 39     | UnlockedManual             | Lock was rotated manually into unlocked position                                                                   |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 40     | Jammed                     | Lock got stuck during locking, unlocking or pull spring action                                                     |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 41     | PowerOff                   | Lock registered long button push and it will power off                                                             |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 42     | PowerOn                    | Lock registered power up                                                                                           |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 43     | Calibration                | User successfully calibrated or recalibrated the lock                                                              |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 46     | BatteryCharging            | Device detected start of charging process                                                                          |

| 47     | PartiallyOpenManual        | Lock was rotated manually from locked or unlocked position into partially locked position                          |

| 48     | PartiallyOpenButton        | Locking or unlocking performed by the button on lock device failed and lock stopped in partially locked position   |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 49     | PartiallyOpenByAutoLock    | Auto lock feature failed and lock stopped in partially locked position                                             |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 50     | BatteryStopCharging        | Device detected end of charging process                                                                            |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 51     | PulledRemote               | Spring was pulled via mobile app                                                                                   |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 52     | PulledButton               | Spring was pulled after pressing the button on lock device, regardless of lock position (no user assigned)         |

| 53     | PulledManual               | Lock was rotated manually to perform pull spring action                                                            |
+--------+----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 54     | PartiallyOpenRemote        | Mobile app sent lock or unlock request and lock stopped in partially locked position                               |

| 55     | PulledAutoByRemote         | Lock was unlocked from locked position via mobile app or by auto unlock feature and spring was pulled              |

| 60     | PulledHomeKit              | Spring was pulled via HomeKit app                                                                                  |

| 61     | UnlockedByPin              | Unlocked from keypad by PIN                                                                                        |
